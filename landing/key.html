Bought it on the Mac App Store?

Then you don't pay again. The App Store version is no longer sold or updated; switch to the version from this website for free:

  1. Find your receipt: the email from Apple, or App Store › your name › Account Settings › Purchase History.
  2. Forward that invoice, or send a screenshot of it, to hello@claudeswitch.dev.
  3. You get a key by email, usually the same day. Download the app, open it and paste the key. Your profiles come over from the App Store version on their own.

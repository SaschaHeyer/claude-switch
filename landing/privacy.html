Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Short version: the Switch for Claude app collects nothing. It has no account and no analytics, and checks your license key on your Mac. Everything it does happens on your Mac. When you buy it, Stripe handles the payment and we keep your email to send your key. This website counts visits without cookies, see below.

What the app reads

To list and open your Claude profiles, Switch for Claude reads folders on your Mac. (The Mac App Store version asks you to grant it your home folder once.) It looks at:

  • Claude Desktop data folders, such as ~/Library/Application Support/Claude-Work, to see whether a profile exists and whether it's open.
  • Claude Code config folders, such as ~/.claude-work, including the account email and plan saved there, so it can show which account each profile uses.
  • Profile lists made by other tools, such as Multi-Claude, so you can bring those profiles over.

The app never reads your sign-in tokens or passwords. Those stay in your macOS Keychain, managed by Claude itself.

What the app stores

Your list of profiles (names, colors and folder locations) is saved inside the app's own folder on your Mac. When you create a profile, the app creates its folders in your home folder. If you choose it, it adds a claude-<name> shortcut to your ~/.zshrc file.

What leaves your Mac

No personal data. The only connection Switch for Claude makes on its own is the update check: about once a day it downloads the list of versions from claudeswitch.dev, which tells our server your IP address, the app version and your macOS version, like any download. Nothing is stored from it beyond ordinary server logs. You can turn the check off in the app's settings. Your license key is checked on your Mac and never sent anywhere. When you open a profile, it starts Claude Desktop or Claude Code, which connect to Anthropic as they always do under Anthropic's own privacy policy.

This website

When you arrive on claudeswitch.dev, the page sends one visit to our statistics service on Google Cloud in the EU. It contains the page you landed on, the page that linked to it (without its query string), any campaign tags in the link, your browser's language and time zone, and your browser's user agent, from which we derive browser, operating system and device type. We use this to see how many people visit and how they find us.

No cookie is set and nothing is stored in your browser. To count unique visitors, the service turns your IP address and user agent into a hash with a secret key that includes the date, so the same browser counts once per day and can't be recognised the next day. The IP address itself is not stored. Visit records are deleted after 24 months.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Moving between pages and reloading a page are not counted.

Purchases

On this website you buy Switch for Claude through Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland), which handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. We receive and keep your email address, your country, the amount, the time and Stripe's IDs for the payment. We use them to create your license key and send it to you, to send it again when you ask, to handle refunds, and to write to you about the app you bought, for example about an update or a problem. You can tell us at any time that you don't want those emails. We keep purchase records as long as tax and commercial law require (up to 10 years). The legal basis is the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)); emails about the app you bought rest on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

The license email is sent through Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), from its EU region. Transfers to the US rest on the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses.

If you bought Switch for Claude on the Mac App Store, Apple handled the payment and gave us no personal details. We only learn your email if you write to us for a key.

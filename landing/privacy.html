switch for claude

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Short version: Switch for Claude collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics and no servers. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

What the app reads

To list and open your Claude profiles, Switch for Claude reads folders on your Mac. You grant it access to your home folder once, when you first open the app. It looks at:

  • Claude Desktop data folders, such as ~/Library/Application Support/Claude-Work, to see whether a profile exists and whether it's open.
  • Claude Code config folders, such as ~/.claude-work, including the account email and plan saved there, so it can show which account each profile uses.
  • Profile lists made by other tools, such as Multi-Claude, so you can bring those profiles over.

The app never reads your sign-in tokens or passwords. Those stay in your macOS Keychain, managed by Claude itself.

What the app stores

Your list of profiles (names, colors and folder locations) is saved inside the app's own folder on your Mac. When you create a profile, the app creates its folders in your home folder. If you choose it, it adds a claude-<name> shortcut to your ~/.zshrc file.

What leaves your Mac

Nothing. Switch for Claude makes no network connections of its own. When you open a profile, it starts Claude Desktop or Claude Code, which connect to Anthropic as they always do under Anthropic's own privacy policy.

Purchases

You buy Switch for Claude through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we receive no personal details about you from it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
